Released [sdl-workflows 1.1.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23875743) with the [SDL Workflows Explorer](https://ccy5123.github.io/sdl-workflows-explorer/), a web view of its 279 platforms from 237 papers: each platform's modules, steps and connections, comparisons across platforms, trends by year, the taxonomy and the benchmark. Version 1.1.0 adds the step times, batch sizes and throughputs the papers state.
